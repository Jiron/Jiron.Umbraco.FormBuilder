## In one paragraph

You may use Jiron Form Builder on your own sites and on the sites you build, host or maintain for clients, free of
charge, and you may change it for those projects. You may not sell it, and you may not publish your own version of it
(free or paid, open source or not) or offer any other product or service that competes with it. Improvements are
welcome in this repository instead.

## Using it

### Can I use it on my company's own website?

Yes, including commercially: on a shop, an intranet, a lead form or anything else your company runs. The licence
lets you do everything with the software that you need to for any permitted purpose, and running your own site is
one.

### Can my agency use it on client sites?

Yes. Installing it on a client's Umbraco site, designing their forms, connecting their actions and maintaining the site
are what it is made for, and you may charge your client for that work. The licence covers your client too: whoever
uses the software under these terms has their own licence from the licensor, so the client can keep using it if
another agency takes over.

### Can I host sites that use it?

Yes. Hosting an Umbraco site that has Jiron Form Builder installed, for yourself or for clients, is using it. What you
may not do is offer Jiron Form Builder itself as a service to others, for example a hosted form builder that people
sign up for instead of installing the package: that would be a product competing with it.

### Do I have to pay, register or show a credit on my site?

No. There is no fee, no registration and no credit on the website. If you give copies of the software to someone
else, see "What do I have to pass on?" below.

## Changing it

### Can I change the code for my own projects?

Yes. You may change it and build new works on it for any permitted purpose: a field type your site needs, a different
look, a fix you can't wait for. Keep the changes to the sites you use it on, your own or your clients'.

### Can I build add-ons?

Add-ons that extend Jiron Form Builder through its documented extension points ([extending.md](extending.md)) and need
it to work don't stand in for it, which is what the licence is about. Building them for your own sites and your
clients' sites is fine. An add-on that contains Jiron Form Builder's own code, or that offers its functionality without
it, is a different matter: it would compete. If you plan to publish or sell an add-on, write to info@ligata.ch first,
so you know where you stand before you invest in it.

## Sharing it

### Can I contribute?

Yes, through this repository: report bugs and suggest features as issues, and once outside contributions open,
propose changes as pull requests from a fork. A fork you keep for contributing back is not a published version of
your own. Outside pull requests can be accepted only once a contributor licence agreement exists, which is being
prepared; [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) says how it works today.

### Why can't I publish my own version, even for free?

Because it would compete with Jiron Form Builder. The licence counts a product as competing if it is marketed as a
substitute for the software's functionality or value, however it is built or delivered, and even when it is free of
charge. The aim is one well-kept Jiron Form Builder that everyone benefits from, rather than copies drifting apart:
an improvement made in this repository reaches every site that installs it. This holds whether your version would be
paid or free, open source or not.

### Can I sell it, or bundle it in something I sell?

You may not sell Jiron Form Builder itself, on its own or renamed. Charging clients for your work on their sites is
fine, and so is a site you deliver with Jiron Form Builder installed. Selling a product whose point is to provide
form building, built from Jiron Form Builder, is not.

### What do I have to pass on when I give someone a copy?

Anyone who gets a copy of the software from you must also get the licence terms (or their address,
<https://polyformproject.org/licenses/perimeter/1.0.1>) and the `Required Notice:` line from `LICENSE.md`. Installing
the NuGet package on a client's site does this for you: the package carries `LICENSE.md`.

## Other questions

### Is it open source?

No. Jiron Form Builder is free and source-available: you can read all of its code and use and change it as described
here, but the licence forbids competing products, which an open-source licence in the OSI sense would not.

### What if I break the licence by mistake?

The licence gives you a way back: the first time you are told in writing that you broke its terms, your licence
continues if you stop and put things right within 32 days.

### Is there a warranty?

No. As far as the law allows, the software comes as is, without any warranty, and the licensor is not liable for
damages. Test it on your sites before you rely on it.

### What about patents?

The licence includes a patent licence for the software. It ends for you if you claim in writing that the software
infringes a patent.

Write to info@ligata.ch, the licensing contact named in [`LICENSE.md`](../LICENSE.md), before you start anything you
are unsure about.
