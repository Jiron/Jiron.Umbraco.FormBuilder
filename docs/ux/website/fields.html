Marking required questions instead

When most questions are optional, an editor can mark the required ones with an asterisk; the form then explains the mark once, above the first question. This form is German and has six pages. Its step names are long, and where one would have to break inside a word, the steps fold into "Schritt 1 von 6" with short bars, and the page title shows.
