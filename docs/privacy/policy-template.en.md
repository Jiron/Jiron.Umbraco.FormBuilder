## Forms on this website

When you fill in a form on this website, we receive and store the answers you give. Which answers a form asks for is
shown on the form itself; answers marked as required are needed for [the purpose of the form]; all others are
voluntary.

**Who is responsible.** [Name, address and contact details of the controller; the data protection officer's contact
details, where one is appointed.]

**What we store with your answers.** The date and time you sent the form, the language you used, the address of the page
the form was on (without anything after a "?"), how long you took to fill it in, the result of our spam check, and, if
you are signed in as a member of this website, which member you are. [If a form records context values: the [names of
the values, for example the campaign that brought you to the page].] [Delete the following sentence if forms keep no IP
address.] We also store [your IP address shortened to the network you used, so that it no longer points to your own
connection | your full IP address], to [purpose, for example to investigate abuse of our forms].

We do not set cookies for our forms and store nothing in your browser[, except for the captcha described below]. [If
"Save and continue later" is offered: When you open a link to continue a form you saved, your browser keeps the link's
code for this tab until you send the form.]

**Why, and on what legal basis.** [For each form or kind of form: the purpose, and the legal basis, for example:
- Contact form: to answer your enquiry; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, or Art.
  6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry concerns a contract.
- Application form: to carry out the application process; Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and [national employment law, for example
  § 26 BDSG].
- Newsletter sign-up: to send you our newsletter; your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.]

**Consent.** Where we ask for your consent, we store that you gave it, the exact wording you agreed to and when. You can
withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, for example by writing to [contact]. [For newsletters:
Your subscription only becomes active once you confirm it through the link we email you, so that no one can sign you up
with your address.]

**Email.** [If forms send emails: After you send a form, we may send you a confirmation and inform the people who handle
your enquiry by email. These emails are kept for 30 days after they were sent and are then deleted. Some answers are
kept only until these emails and our other follow-up steps are done, and are then deleted.]

**Spam protection.** To tell people from automated misuse, the form asks your browser to solve a small task when you
first use it; this involves only our own server. [If you use a captcha provider: For [form], we use [reCAPTCHA by Google
Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland | hCaptcha by Intuition Machines, Inc., 350 Alabama St,

The provider receives the information its script collects in your browser, which may include your IP address, and its
script may set cookies and store data in your browser; our server then sends the provider the code its check gave your
browser, so the provider can confirm it, without your IP address. Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and
§ 25(1) TDDDG. [Transfers to the USA: describe the safeguard, for example the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard
contractual clauses.]]

**How long we keep your answers.** We keep the answers to [form] for [number] days after [it was last handled | it was
approved | it was rejected], and then delete them automatically, unless we must keep them longer by law; in that case we
keep them only as long as required. [Answers sent to [form] are not stored at all once our follow-up steps are done.]
Statistics about our forms (such as how many people opened a form) contain no personal data.

**Who receives your data.** The people in our organisation who handle [the form's purpose]. Our website is hosted by
[hosting provider], and our emails are sent through [email provider]; both process your data on our behalf under a data
processing agreement. [Further recipients, for example a CRM or newsletter service that a form passes your answers to,
and whether data leaves the EU or Switzerland.]

**Your rights.** You have the right to access the data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict
its processing, to receive it in a common electronic format, and to object to processing based on our legitimate
interests. Where processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. You can also complain to a data
protection supervisory authority, [for example the one where you live, or ours: name and address]. To exercise your
rights, write to [contact].
