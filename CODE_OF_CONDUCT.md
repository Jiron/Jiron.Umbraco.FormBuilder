# Code of conduct

Jiron Form Builder adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, as its code of conduct. The full text is
published at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>, and it applies here as
written there, with the contact details below.

## In short

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews and any other
project space, is welcome regardless of who they are. We expect people to be kind and patient, to respect
different views and experiences, to give and take feedback gracefully, and to own their mistakes.
Harassment, insults, unwelcome attention, publishing others' private information and any other conduct that
would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not accepted.

The code of conduct also applies when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks the code of conduct to **info@ligata.ch**. Every report is reviewed promptly
and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and safety are respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer enforces the code of conduct and may remove, edit or reject contributions that break it. The
consequences follow the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1: a correction, a warning, a
temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on the impact and on whether the behaviour continues.
